AI Solutions CRM

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

AI Solutions CRM is an internal business tool used by AI Solutions and its team members to manage client projects, record working hours and prepare payroll reports. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

Information the app stores

  • Team member details entered by an administrator: name, location, email address, phone number, role, hourly rate, currency, access level and an optional profile photo.
  • Project details: client name, project name, tasks and hour limits.
  • Timesheet entries: date, hours worked, project, task, notes and approval status.
  • A hashed version of each person's sign-in PIN. The PIN itself is never stored.

Where it is stored

Information is saved in the app's storage on each device so the app works offline. When a device is connected to the company's backend, the same information is stored in a Google Sheet owned by AI Solutions, using Google Apps Script. Google processes that data under the company's Google account terms.

How it is used

The information is used only to run the app: showing projects and hours, checking hour limits, approving timesheets and calculating pay. Weekly reports (PDF and Excel) containing team names, hours and pay are emailed to the company's nominated address through Google.

What the app does not do

The app has no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share information with anyone outside AI Solutions. To display correctly, the app loads fonts, icons and document tools from Google Fonts and the cdnjs content network, which receive standard connection details such as your IP address.

Access, correction and deletion

Administrators can view, correct or remove team member and timesheet information in the app at any time. Team members can ask an administrator to do so. To have your information removed from a device, sign out and use "Reset this device" on the sign-in screen.
